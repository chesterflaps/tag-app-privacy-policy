Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, use the account deletion feature in the Settings tab of the app. This will permanently remove all your TAGs and data from our servers.

Device Information

When contacting support, please include:

  • Android version (e.g., Android 13)
  • TAG App version (found in Settings)
  • Description of the issue you're experiencing

Common Android Issues

Contact Sync Issues

If contacts aren't syncing properly:

  1. Go to Android Settings > Apps > TAG > Permissions
  2. Ensure "Contacts" permission is enabled
  3. Try visiting the Contacts tab in the app to trigger a sync

Google Sign-In Problems

If you can't sign in with Google:

  1. Check your internet connection
  2. Ensure you're signed into your Google account on your device
  3. Try clearing the app's cache in Settings > Apps > TAG > Storage
