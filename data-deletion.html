🗑️ Delete Your TAG Account

TAG App - Account Deletion Request

Follow the steps below to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

⚠️ Important Notice

Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data from TAG. This action cannot be undone.

Please make sure you want to delete your account before proceeding. All your custom tags, preferences, and account information will be permanently lost.

📋 What Data Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • All your custom tags - Every tag you've created will be permanently deleted
  • Tag associations - All phone number to tag mappings will be removed
  • User preferences - Your app settings and contact sync preferences will be deleted
  • Account information - Your Google account association will be removed
  • Purchase history - Records of any in-app purchases will be deleted

📱 How to Delete Your Account

Step 1: Open the TAG app on your Android device
Step 2: Go to the "Settings" tab (bottom right corner)
Step 3: Scroll down to find the "Delete Account" button
Step 4: Tap "Delete Account" and read the confirmation dialog
Step 5: Confirm your choice by tapping "Delete" in the dialog
Step 6: Your account and all data will be permanently deleted

⏰ Data Retention Policy

Once you delete your account:

  • Your data is immediately and permanently deleted
  • No data is retained for any period
  • No backups are kept
  • The deletion is irreversible
  • You will need to create a new account if you want to use TAG again
